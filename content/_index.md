This is the OTIS unit wiki, where anyone who can figure out
how to make a pull request on GitHub (a strong minority!) can contribute.
Also, there are some [autogenerated statistics](@/info/statistics.md).
It describes the curriculum of [OTIS](https://web.evanchen.cc/otis.html)
in fandom-level detail.

Most pages were created by students in 2022-2023 on an internal class wiki,
so the style and tone of the pages is that of past students.
You can use it to get an idea of what students from back then thought
of the unit, what it's about, and what kind of problems to expect.
In 2025-2026, Evan split off the wiki to a dedicated domain,
`catalog.evanchen.cc`, which is publicly viewable --- you're reading it now.

If you spot anything out of date,
or want to make improvements or changes,
please [file a pull request](https://github.com/vEnhance/catalog.evanchen.cc)!
Revisions to the wiki would be really welcome,
particularly filling in [unfinished articles](@/info/contributing.md).
