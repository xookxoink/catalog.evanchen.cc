Each page provides a snapshot of some statistics for each of the groups of units.
These statistics are explained below:

- **Participants**: The number of students, over all years,
  who have this unit on their dashboard.
- **Submissions**: The number of students, over all years,
  who have submitted this unit.
- **♣ earned**: In general, problems in each unit can be worth 2, 3, 5, or 9 clubs.
  Students complete each unit by passing a certain threshold.
  This statistic counts the total number of clubs earned over all submissions.
- **❤ earned**: Students can self-report approximately how many hours they
  spent on each unit.
  This statistic sums the total number of hours spent over all submissions.
  (Obviously, since this is a self-reported statistic,
  it's meant to just be a ballpark reference point.)

All of these are counted with multiplicity over all
[versions](@/info/unit-codes.md) of the unit.

The statistics are updated periodically.
